# Sokrates: AI Interview Copilot Guide

Sokrates is an AI interview copilot for macOS and Windows. It listens to both sides of a live call, transcribes in real time, and streams short answers into a private overlay excluded from screen sharing. It also includes scored mock interviews, a meeting mode with recaps, and a phone companion. There's a free plan, and no card is needed.

**Website:** [sokrates.cloud](https://www.sokrates.cloud) · **Support:** support@sokrates.cloud

## Deploy

1. Copy `sokrates-ai-interview-copilot.html` to your site, e.g. as `/guides/ai-interview-copilot/index.html`.
2. If you use a different path, update the URL in three places: `<link rel="canonical">`, `og:url` and `mainEntityOfPage` in the Article JSON-LD.
3. Optional: match your brand colors by editing `--accent` in the `:root` block and again in the dark-mode block.
4. Deploy, then submit the URL in [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) and add it to your sitemap.

## Editing the content

The visible FAQ and the `FAQPage` JSON-LD in `<head>` must stay identical. If you change a question or answer in one, change it in the other.

Prices, plan limits and version numbers reflect the product as of 29 October 2026. Update them in the pricing table, the "Choosing" table and the FAQ whenever they change.

## Brand voice

Short, confident sentences, with numbers over adjectives and honesty about limits. Describe what the overlay does ("excluded from screen capture"). Never use "undetectable", "cheat" or similar wording.
