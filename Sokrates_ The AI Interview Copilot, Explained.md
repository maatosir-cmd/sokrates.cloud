## Why Sokrates is great

Sokrates gives you a usable answer while the question is still landing, in a shape you can use mid-sentence. Alt-tabbing to a chatbot is slow, shows on a shared screen, and returns a wall of text you can't read while talking. Sokrates is built for the forty minutes that decide it.

### Live transcription with AI answers, from both sides of the call

Sokrates records your mic and your system audio as two separate, labeled channels: Interviewer and You. Both update word by word. With the mic on, it drafts an answer as each question lands, so you don't press anything.

It works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, browser calls and phone calls routed through your computer: anything that plays sound. No bot joins the call, and nothing gets installed in the meeting app.

### Answers shaped for a live conversation

Every answer has a fixed shape: a few bullets to glance at (Scan), then a first-person paragraph you can read aloud (Say). That is what makes Sokrates an interview answer generator you can actually use while you're talking.

| Question type | What you see on screen |
| --- | --- |
| Concept, general or ML | Scan: 3–5 bullets of up to 8 words, direct answer first. Say: 50–90 words, first person |
| Coding | Say: a one-line approach. Code: a full, runnable solution. Explain: time and space complexity in 2 bullets |
| System design | Design: 6–8 one-line stages. Say: your scoping assumption and headline architecture, with a number. Then the trade-offs |
| Behavioral | Four STAR beats, then a first-person story drawn from your own résumé |

Scan to glance at. Say to read aloud.

### Four ways to ask

- **Automatic:** answers draft as the interviewer speaks.
- **Ask from voice or Answer now:** get an answer on demand, the moment you want it.
- **Type:** the chat box takes a typed question, with the recent transcript as context.
- **Snap (screenshot to answer):** Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+S turns an on-screen coding or design prompt into an answer.

### Your résumé, your job description, your voice

Sokrates uses your résumé and job description, plus your notes, as context. Set the company, role and round type for the session. Answers match that seniority. They never claim a job or project that isn't in your material.

A "Tell Sokrates how to answer" box on the start screen shapes every answer's tone, emphasis and what to avoid. File uploads are part of Pro.

### Answer length control and Go Deeper

Choose Short, Standard or Long in Settings → Answers, or from your phone. When the interviewer follows up, Go Deeper continues the answer in your voice. In a system design round, it walks the next stage the interviewer is likely to ask about.

### A private overlay, hidden from screen sharing

The desktop AI overlay is excluded from screen capture by the operating system itself: NSWindow sharingType on macOS, SetWindowDisplayAffinity on Windows. It doesn't appear in a shared screen or a recording. It stays off the taskbar and has an opacity slider and a Ghost mode.

Keyboard shortcuts work while the call app has focus (Cmd on Mac, Ctrl on Windows): Shift+A asks from voice, Shift+S snaps, Shift+M toggles the mic, and Shift+\\ hides or shows the overlay.

### Phone companion app

Open [sokrates.cloud/companion](https://www.sokrates.cloud/companion) in Safari or Chrome; there's no app store download. Paired with the desktop, your phone mirrors answers under your webcam, and its buttons trigger Answer now, Snap, Go Deeper, Hide and Clear on the laptop.

On its own, the phone listens through its mic, photographs the laptop screen, attaches documents and runs practice. The screen is true black, has a brightness slider and larger text, and stays on for the whole session.

## How Sokrates helps you prepare and ace the interview

Sokrates covers the whole loop: rehearse with scored mock interviews, get live help in every round, then review what to tighten. Rehearse it before it counts.

### Before the interview: a mock interview with AI

Practice mode is an interviewer who never runs out of questions. Pick a round, a difficulty and your focus areas. A narrator voice asks one question at a time, out loud, with no small talk.

You answer out loud too, the way you will in the room. That makes it AI interview practice with spoken questions, not a question bank, and the most direct way to practice behavioral questions out loud. After each answer, you get:

- a score out of 10 and a verdict that quotes your own words
- what Worked, what to Improve and what you Missed
- a model answer

| Score | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| 8 or more | Clear hire signal |
| 6–7 | Borderline |
| 5 | Would not pass at this level |
| 3 or less | Skipped, or a one-line answer |

Rounds include general/mixed, intro/recruiter, behavioral (STAR), coding, system design, ML/data science, HR/culture fit and custom. Behavioral, intro, HR and general rounds ask about your own résumé; coding, design and ML rounds bring fresh problems. It's interview feedback AI that's like the real thing, with the feedback you never get.

### System design interview help

AI for system design interviews has to follow how the round actually runs. Sokrates opens with a scoping assumption and a headline architecture with a number, then lays out 6–8 one-line stages and the trade-offs. System design gets a staged walkthrough, not code.

When the interviewer drills in, Go Deeper walks the next stage they're likely to ask about. If the prompt is written on screen, Snap it with Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+S.

### Behavioral interviews and STAR answers

Behavioral rounds reward structure and brevity. Sokrates works as a STAR method answer generator built from your own résumé: four STAR beats to glance at, then a first-person story to tell. It picks the project that fits the question, so you get to the point instead of losing the interviewer in the backstory.

Because answers never claim work that isn't in your material, your behavioral interview STAR answers stay true to your record.

### Coding interviews and live coding rounds

As a coding interview assistant, Sokrates starts with the problem in front of you. Snap the prompt the moment it appears and you get a one-line approach to say, a full runnable solution, and time and space complexity in two bullets.

Live coding round help is about direction: confirming the approach early keeps you off a dead-end path for twenty minutes.

### Technical, ML and data science interviews

As a technical interview copilot, Sokrates gives concept and ML questions the Scan-then-Say shape: the direct answer first in 3–5 bullets, then 50–90 words to say. For data analyst and data science interview help, it covers ML concepts and ML system design live, and Practice has a dedicated ML/data science round.

PM loops lean hard on behavioral rounds. As a product manager interview assistant, Sokrates builds STAR stories from your own résumé and keeps them short enough to land. Use the "Tell Sokrates how to answer" box to set emphasis, such as leading with metrics.
